# Decision: slice conserved budget core before implementation

The authoritative budget design is not one reviewable task. Use a producer-first sequence: contract -> hierarchical ledger -> protected reservations, with measurement truth parallel after contract -> settlement -> held-out randomized/concurrency hardening. Every production/test TS stays <=250 lines and every implementation child stays at three paths including its JS bridge. Keep scheduler budget internal and dependency-free until ownership explicitly includes scheduler package.json, pnpm-lock.yaml, and src/index.ts; never relative-import core outside scheduler rootDir or duplicate policy evaluation. UNKNOWN quantity remains null/UNKNOWN, not zero, and protected reserves always include verification, independent review, final acceptance, and contingency with caller-supplied amounts.
