## Design decisions a reviewer will question

- **Six bound fields, not seven.** DoD 2 names exactly criteria/hashes/tree/
  receipts/rubric/submittedBytes and the plan demands set-equality against that
  list. Design 15.2 prose also mentions "prior structured review findings" — those
  live in the append-only lineage (`review-findings.ts`), not as a 7th binding.
  Documented in the `review-contract.ts` header.
- **Independence is 3-valued**, never boolean+throw. `UNKNOWN` is a first-class
  verdict that cannot accept.
- **Exclusions by allow-list, not filter.** `REVIEW_PACKAGE_FORBIDDEN_ITEM_KINDS`
  is only a backstop that upgrades `PACKAGE_ITEM_KIND_UNKNOWN` to
  `..._FORBIDDEN`. Deleting it tightens nothing. Do not "simplify" it into a
  deny-list.
- **Receipts are opaque 64-hex, shape-checked only.** Evidence receipt pipeline
  (task-1e512b95) is unbuilt and owns their internals.
- **No journal/transcript import anywhere.** You cannot import a thing to leave it out.
- **Policy is consumed, never restated.** `qualifyReviewAcceptance` calls
  `@moe/core`'s `evaluatePolicy` and carries its `reasonCodes` verbatim.
  `@moe/core`'s exports map is `"."` only, so `validateApprovalRecord` is NOT
  reachable — go through `evaluatePolicy` + the record's core-owned `validity`.
