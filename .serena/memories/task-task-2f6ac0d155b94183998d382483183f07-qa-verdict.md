## Raised to the architect, NOT held against this task
`decideApproval` binds only `record.exactRevisionHash` to durable state. The rest of the approval
record — `actorKind: "HUMAN"`, `truthClass: "HUMAN_APPROVED"`, `riskTier`, `applicablePolicyRef` —
is caller-supplied, so anyone who can send bytes can mint a HUMAN_APPROVED decision against a
durably proposed plan. Outside this task's DoD (DoD 1 is activation-only; DoD 3 names four inputs,
and wrong-hash is covered) and unfixable inside it, since no durable approval-request surface
exists among its dependencies. Needs a follow-up task that sources the record from the store.
