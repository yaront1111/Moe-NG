# Whole-launcher seams can forge PROVEN authority

If a production service accepts an injected function for the entire runner launch boundary and trusts a returned `kind/truthClass`, tests or callers can forge `OBSERVED/PROVEN` without consuming a grant, observing PREFLIGHT/STARTED, or recording immutable process evidence.

Keep seams below the authority-producing surface (transport/process primitives), or validate/capture the real runner result and its required observation/registration evidence. Add a negative test whose injected result omits required evidence and assert the exact stable code/layer.
